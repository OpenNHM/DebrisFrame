Glossary
========

.. Note:: **This is still under construction**

.. Note:: The main purpose of this glossary is to introduce and consolidate an
   unambiguous language and definitions related to debris-flow simulations
   and modeling for the simulation framework DebrisFrame.

.. glossary::
    :sorted:

    cross section
        Section perpendicular to the main direction of flow bounded by the free surface and wetted perimeter of the stream or channel.

    debris-flow hydrograph
        A debris-flow hydrograph (in German Murgangline) is the time series
        describing how the :term:`discharge` of a debris flow varies at a given cross-section.
        Unlike a conventional :term:`hydrograph`, a debris-flow hydrograph represents
        a mixture of water, sediment, and sometimes boulders and woody debris.
        A debris-flow hydrograph can be used as a starting condition for a debris-flow simulation.

    discharge
        Volume of water or debris flowing through a channel cross-section per unit time.

    entrainment
        Entrainment refers to the incorporation of material into a moving debris flow through the :term:`erosion` of the
        bed, resulting in an increase in flowing mass.

    erosion
        Erosion is the process by which material is detached and removed from the bed surface due to the forces exerted
        by the flowing debris mass.

    hydrograph
        A hydrograph is the time series describing how the :term:`discharge` of water varies at a given cross-section.
